Privacy Policy

This app does not collect any personal information from the device. It only requests permission to block other apps and does not store or transmit user data.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Aether Focus operates entirely on-device. No telemetry, identity records, keystrokes, contact lists, or browsing histories are recorded, uploaded, or transmitted to any remote servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

2. Purpose of Device Permissions

All permissions requested by Aether Focus are strictly intended to provide app-blocking and local screen-time analysis features configured by the user:

  • Device Administrator: Prevents bypassing active restriction intervals or unauthorized app deletion during focus periods.
  • Accessibility Service: Detects when a restricted application enters the foreground to display the focus shield overlay immediately.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Reads local foreground application usage durations solely to compute daily/weekly screen-time analytics against user-specified benchmarks.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Renders the full-screen restriction shield over blocked applications.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: Ensures scheduled downtime intervals and timers execute without unexpected background termination by Android OS.

3. Local Storage

User preferences, customized schedules, habit tracker checkoffs, star balances, and ledger history are stored exclusively in encrypted on-device SharedPreferences and are completely erased whenever the user clears app data or uninstalls the application.
